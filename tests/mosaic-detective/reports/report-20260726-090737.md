**Evidence:**
- Telemetry pipeline is healthy — `otel-collector` and `prometheus` are UP; only the expected `vllm-*` targets are down.
- Collective throughput (`nccl_profiler_collective_bytes_total`) is active and uniform across all 4 ranks (delta ratio 1.00 everywhere) — no dead rank, no stalled communicator.
- `DCGM_FI_DEV_SM_CLOCK` on **golf, GPU 1 (rank 3)** dipped as low as **735 MHz** (vs. a steady ~2805 MHz baseline on all peers) between **08:52:54 and 09:06:09** today — a clock ratio of ~0.26 at the trough, consistent with a thermal/power clamp on that one GPU.
- The clamp has since **cleared**: the anomaly's last transition (09:06:09) returns the rate to 0, and both the 2-minute and narrowed 1-minute windows now show all 4 ranks at parity (ratio 1.00, mean ~2805–2820 MHz).

**Ruled out:**
- Collector/telemetry outage — collector is up and counters are actively advancing.
- Dead rank or communicator stall — all ranks show identical, non-zero throughput deltas.
- Network/interconnect degradation — not investigated further since the GPU-clock signature is already conclusive and current.

**Bottom line:** GPU 1 on host `golf` (rank 3) experienced a clock clamp (down to ~26% of normal SM clock) for roughly 13 minutes ending at 09:06 today (2026-07-26). It has since recovered to normal and the cluster is currently healthy. Worth checking `golf`'s thermal/power logs around 08:52–09:06 if this recurs.
