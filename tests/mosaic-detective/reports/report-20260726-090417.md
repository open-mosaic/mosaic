## Diagnosis: GPU clock clamp on rank 3 (golf)

**Evidence:**
- Telemetry pipeline healthy — `otel-collector` UP, NCCL counters actively advancing on all 4 ranks (only the `vllm-*` targets are down, which is normal idle state, not a fault).
- Collective bytes delta over the last 2 minutes: all 4 ranks uniform at ratio 1.00 — no rank has stopped or is lagging in NCCL terms.
- `DCGM_FI_DEV_SM_CLOCK` mean over the last 2 minutes: ranks 0, 1, 2 at ~2805–2820 MHz (ratio ~1.00), but **rank 3 (golf) at 742 MHz — ratio 0.26**.

**Ruled out:**
- Not a telemetry gap (collector is up, counters are advancing).
- Not a dead rank / completed job (no counter freeze — all ranks are still growing at the same rate).
- Not network degradation — that signature requires *all* ranks' SM clocks to look normal with only aggregate throughput down. Here one GPU's clock is clearly singled out.

**Conclusion:** rank 3's GPU on host `golf` is clock-clamped to ~26% of its peers' frequency (742 MHz vs. ~2810 MHz). Because all-reduce synchronizes ranks, the whole communicator is being dragged down to that GPU's pace even though NCCL-level metrics look uniform — the clamp only shows up in the per-GPU DCGM clock data, not in collective throughput deltas. This is an active, current condition, not a resolved one.
