## Diagnosis: GPU clock clamp on `golf`, rank 3

**Fault:** The GPU on host `golf` backing rank 3 (device nvidia1, PCI `0000:A2:00.0`) dropped its SM clock from ~2790 MHz to 742 MHz — a clamp to ~26% of its normal clock, and still active now.

**Evidence:**
- Telemetry pipeline is healthy (`otel-collector` up, only the expected `vllm-*` targets down).
- `nccl_profiler_collective_bytes_total` is advancing identically across all 4 ranks (~9.07e9 bytes over 2 min each) — collectives are still running, ruling out a dead/hung rank process.
- `DCGM_FI_DEV_SM_CLOCK` shows ranks 0, 1, 2 steady at ~2805–2820 MHz, while rank 3 sits at 742 MHz (ratio 0.26 vs peers).
- The 15-minute anomaly scan pinpoints the drop to **2026-07-26 10:10:22–10:10:37**, a sharp step down that has not recovered — the clamp is ongoing.

**Ruled out:** telemetry/collector failure, a stalled or crashed rank, and network/interconnect degradation (NCCL byte throughput is uniform, not reduced) — this is a GPU-level clock clamp on golf's rank-3 device, likely thermal/power throttling, dragging the whole all-reduce down to its pace since collectives synchronize on the slowest rank.
