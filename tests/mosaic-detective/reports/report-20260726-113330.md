## Diagnosis: GPU clock clamp on golf, rank 3

**Fault:** The GPU at `golf` (device nvidia1, rank 3, PCI `A2:00.0`) has its SM clock clamped, dropping from a steady 2790 MHz to 742 MHz starting at **2026-07-26 11:32:20**, and it remains clamped now (currently 742 MHz vs. ~2805–2820 MHz on the other three GPUs — a ratio of 0.26).

**Evidence:**
- Telemetry pipeline is healthy — only the (expected/ignorable) `vllm-*` targets are down.
- `nccl_profiler_collective_bytes_total` looks uniform across all 4 ranks (~1.00 ratio) — this is a red herring, since all-reduce synchronizes ranks and masks a single slow GPU.
- `DCGM_FI_DEV_SM_CLOCK` is the metric that localizes it: golf/rank 3 sits at 0.67x peers over 2 minutes, narrowing to 0.26x over 1 minute — confirming an active, worsening clamp rather than a transient blip.
- 15-minute anomaly scan pinpoints the clock drop to 11:32:20, with the other three GPUs (bravo rank 0/1, golf rank 2) showing flat, unchanged clocks throughout.

**Ruled out:** Collector/telemetry failure (pipeline is up and counters are actively advancing), a dead rank process (all 4 ranks are still emitting NCCL traffic), and a network/interconnect issue (NCCL byte throughput is uniform, which wouldn't be the case for a link-level slowdown).

**Bottom line:** One GPU on host `golf` is thermally/power throttled or otherwise clamped, silently dragging down the whole all-reduce group's effective throughput even though NCCL counters look fine.
